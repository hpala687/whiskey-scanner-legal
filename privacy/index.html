Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Whiskey Scanner (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the App.

At a glance: We don’t run ads, we don’t sell data, and label scanning runs on your device. We only receive information if you choose to contact us or if you opt in to sharing diagnostics.

Quick Summary

  • No account, no login, no social features.
  • No ads and no third‑party tracking SDKs.
  • Camera frames and label images are processed on your device. By default, they are not sent to us.
  • Crash reports and basic diagnostics may be collected to improve stability if enabled on your device or if you opt in.
  • In‑app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.
  • We receive your email and any information you include only if you contact us for support.
  • We never sell your data.
  • The App is intended for users at or above the legal drinking age in their country/region.

Information We Collect

On‑device processing (images and camera)

The App uses your device’s camera to scan whiskey labels. Images and camera frames are processed locally to identify text or patterns. By default, we do not receive, store, or transmit these images to our servers.

You can delete any images saved in your photo library at any time using your device controls.

Diagnostics and crash data

To keep the App reliable, we may access anonymized crash logs and basic diagnostics (e.g., device model, OS version, app version) provided by the platform (such as Apple Crash Reports) or by an optional, privacy‑respecting analytics mechanism if you opt in. These diagnostics do not include your contact information or the contents of your scans.

Support communications

If you email us or otherwise contact support, we will receive your name, email address, and any information you include in your message so we can assist you.

Purchases and payments

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple (In‑App Purchase). We receive transaction receipts and limited purchase metadata needed to validate your entitlement, but we do not receive or store your full payment card details. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS under Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

How We Use Information

  • Provide core features like label scanning and results.
  • Troubleshoot issues, fix bugs, and improve performance.
  • Respond to your requests and support inquiries.

Sharing and Disclosure

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Service providers (e.g., email, crash reporting offered by the platform) may process data on our behalf solely to provide their services to us, under appropriate safeguards.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, safety, or the rights and safety of others.

Data Retention

  • Images and scans: processed on‑device and not sent to us by default. Any images you save remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App.
  • Diagnostics: crash and stability data are kept only as long as necessary to investigate and improve the App, typically up to 90 days unless a longer period is needed to resolve an issue.
  • Support emails: retained as long as needed to resolve your request and for reasonable recordkeeping.

Device Permissions

  • Camera: used to scan whiskey labels. You may disable camera access in system settings, although scanning will not function without it.
  • Photo Library (optional): only used if you choose to import or save an image. The App does not access your library unless you explicitly interact with those features.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please avoid sending sensitive information in support emails.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing of your information. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

Children and Age Restrictions

The App is not directed to individuals below the legal drinking age in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect information from such individuals. If you believe someone under the legal drinking age has used the App or contacted us, please reach out so we can remove any related information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App or legal requirements. We will update the “Last updated” date at the top when changes are made.

Contact
